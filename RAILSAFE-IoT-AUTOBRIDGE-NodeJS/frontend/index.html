IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

Prototype disclaimer

This project is a frontend demonstration. All train locations, speeds, ETAs, ESP32 status, limit switches, motor current, bridge position and safety alerts are generated mock values.

It does not connect to railway signalling, official train APIs, GPS hardware or an ESP32. Never use this page to make real-world railway or passenger-safety decisions. A real system needs approved railway engineering, certified fail-safe hardware and independent safety validation.
